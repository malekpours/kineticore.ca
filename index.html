Featured product

KC-36 Thermocouple Logger

A 36-channel K-type thermocouple acquisition unit with real-time visualization, calibrated logging and threshold alarms — built on National Instruments modules for research-grade accuracy in demanding environments.

  • 36 isolated K-type inputs (miniature & standard plugs)
  • Calibrated accuracy of ±0.5 °C typical (0–100 °C range)
  • Live multi-channel graphing, CSV logging and annotation
  • High-durability writes for power-loss protection
