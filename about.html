Our story

From Solving Lab Problems to Solving a Planetary One

Kineticore began as a behind-the-scenes technical partner for research teams — designing and delivering turnkey data acquisition systems so scientists never had to act as system integrators. Every project sharpened the same expertise: making physical processes visible, measurable and trustworthy.

That expertise pointed at a bigger question. If we can instrument any process, why not one of the most important ones — converting waste biomass into stable carbon? Today we operate two divisions with one shared philosophy: if it matters, measure it.
