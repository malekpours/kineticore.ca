Our Services

End-to-end engineering for measurement systems — and process production for the circular bioeconomy. We take ownership of the full technical stack so your team does not lose time assembling, configuring or troubleshooting.

Division one — laboratory engineering

Data Acquisition & Lab System Engineering

For industrial R&D teams and applied research laboratories.

Custom Measurement & DAQ Hardware Systems

Multi-channel sensor systems (temperature, pressure, flow, strain, gas and more), vendor-agnostic architectures, custom enclosures, factory testing and calibration prior to delivery. You receive a fully assembled, validated system — not individual components.

Data Acquisition Software & Visualization

Real-time monitoring, configurable sampling rates and alarms, per-channel metadata and annotations, reliable export for downstream analysis — compatible with Python, MATLAB and other analysis workflows.

System Integration, Connectivity & Security

Integration across sensors, DAQ systems, control hardware and databases. Secure local, networked or hybrid storage; encrypted communication; isolated or air-gapped options; fail-safe strategies for long-duration experiments.

Calibration, Validation & Documentation

Initial calibration and verification, system documentation, clear operating procedures, and recalibration support as projects evolve — keeping your system defensible, repeatable and audit-ready.

Division two — biomass conversion

Pyrolysis & Biochar Services

Our upcoming facility extends our engineering discipline into production. We work with partners across the whole value chain.

Feedstock Partnerships

Sawmills, municipalities and land-clearing operators: convert disposal costs into value. We accept clean wood waste, chips and residues — reliably and with documented chain of custody.

Toll Processing

Have your own biomass? We process it and return biochar or share outputs under contract — with per-batch process data for your own reporting.

Biochar Supply

Agriculture, filtration, construction and remediation customers: certified-quality biochar with per-batch characterization and consistent specification.
